# Characterization coverage: koi

8 of 14 actants carry a characterization (a role read from a frame) in at least one of the 2 frame(s); 6 are bare. A bare actant is context that situates the network (a person, an external org, a platform, an output) without being given a role reading; that is expected, not a gap. This view keeps the balance legible.
