# Inscriptions: pi-learning

0 inscription(s): the case's material carry-forward. Each is **produced** by an origin (`ant:inscribes`) and **drawn on** by consumers (`ant:drawsOn`). The shared object is the thread that persists even when a producing site is wound down.

> **Type = how it persists.** An *immutable mobile* holds form constant and is **unaltered** when drawn on (the invariant, intermediary-like pole); a *fluid object* persists through mutation and may be **altered** when drawn on: a consumer keeps developing the living object (the varied, mediator-like pole).
