## Architectural (chief engineer)

- **Held by:** mzargham
- **Reading:** KOI as constitutive infrastructure
- **Authors:** 1 network · 1 translations · 5 characterizations

The chief-engineer's frame on KOI/RID: reading the assemblage as a constitutive network — realized substrate (referenceable objects, cryptographic identity, capability advertisement, edge negotiation) plus instructed overlays — under the discipline of scoped authority, per-relationship governance, and adopt-don't-mint. Grounded in protocol-design practice.

**Practice · Protocol design:**

The systems-engineering practice of specifying coordination machinery — protocol objects, identifiers, state machines, capability negotiation — as commitments authoritative at the functional layer while remaining agnostic at the physical layer (CFLP discipline). The frame within which the chief engineer reads KOI as realized substrate plus instructed direction.

## Ethnographic (Rennie)

- **Held by:** erennie
- **Reading:** Building the loop in the field
- **Authors:** 1 network · 1 translations · 8 characterizations

Ellie Rennie's frame on the KOI field sites: reading the same infrastructure through participatory digital ethnography — Telescope, KOI Sync, consent flows, and the human interventions and problematizations by which ethnographers and participants are enrolled into (and resist) the loop. Grounded in participatory digital ethnography practice.

**Practice · Participatory digital ethnography:**

The reflexive, participatory practice of studying socio-technical coordination by configuring the instruments that record, filter, and signal what counts as data — observing, interpreting, and inscribing within the feedback loops of the systems studied ('building the loop'; Rennie, Nabben et al.). The frame within which infrastructure is read for what it transforms about organisational knowledge.
