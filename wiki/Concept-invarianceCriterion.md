**Label:** invariance criterion

## Definition

Names the INVARIANT this Characterization tracks: what is held constant (the regulation goal). It is what is PRESERVED regardless of role; the role says HOW: an Intermediary preserves it by pass-through, a Mediator preserves it by regulating (varying OTHER dimensions to keep this invariant — requisite variety). So the reading is 'passes through X' (Intermediary) or 'regulates to preserve X' (Mediator); X is the invariant in both, never the thing varied. An actant may carry two Characterizations at once (Mediator on the dimensions it regulates, Intermediary on those it passes through); the perspective selects which are coded. v1: free-text string. v1.1: candidate reification to ant:Invariance class (structured dimensions). See ADR-0006.
