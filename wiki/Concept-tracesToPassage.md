# tracesToPassage _(Object property)_

[← Home](Home)

**IRI:** `https://w3id.org/ant#tracesToPassage`

**Label:** traces to passage

## Definition

A translation (a commitment / program of action) traces to an obligatory passage point it must clear, named by the actant that is characterized as an OPP (possibly in another frame). Complements ant:passesThrough (whose subject is an Actant).

## Source

Callon, M. (1986), obligatory passage point; ADR-0002.
