# ConstraintWaiver _(Class)_

[← Home](Home)

**IRI:** `https://w3id.org/ant#ConstraintWaiver`

**Label:** Constraint waiver

## Definition

An append-only acknowledgement of a Tier-2 SHACL warning, with required justification and waiver-author. Never applies to Tier-1 Violations (which represent structural integrity). Lets analysts honestly override 'should-conform' constraints without silent violation. See ADR-0000 R9b.
