# correspondsTo _(Object property)_

[← Home](Home)

**IRI:** `https://w3id.org/ant#correspondsTo`

**Label:** corresponds to

## Definition

Cross-frame correspondence between actants that name the same or a tightly-coupled referent read differently by frame. Weaker than identity; symmetric. Cf. skos:closeMatch.
