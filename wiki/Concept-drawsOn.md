# drawsOn _(Object property)_

[← Home](Home)

**IRI:** `https://w3id.org/ant#drawsOn`

**Label:** draws on

## Definition

An actant draws on (consumes, builds upon) an inscription it did not necessarily produce; the consuming complement of ant:inscribes. Drawing on an ant:FluidObject may alter it (the consumer keeps developing the living object), whereas drawing on an ant:ImmutableMobile leaves it unaltered (it is referenced, not changed) — the same invariance/variance distinction that separates a Mediator from an Intermediary, here on the material trace itself. See ADR-0005.

## Source

Synthesized for the Latourian inscription cluster (Latour, B. (1990). Drawing Things Together); de Laet, M. & Mol, A. (2000) for the fluid/immutable variance.
