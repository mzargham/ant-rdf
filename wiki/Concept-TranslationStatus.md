# TranslationStatus _(Class)_

[← Home](Home)

**IRI:** `https://w3id.org/ant#TranslationStatus`

**Label:** Translation status

## Definition

The current standing of a translation read behaviorally: a translation is 'done' not when permanent but when a new behavioral regularity stabilizes; success is that regularity's durability, failure its unravelling. Enumerated by ant:Stabilized / ant:Precarious / ant:Unravelled. A translation with no status is 'forming / not yet assessed' — a deliberate state, not missing data.

## Source

Callon, M. (1986); Law, J. (2008) (precariousness, C5); Zargham, M. & Ben-Meir, I., Protocols & Institutions (behavioral regularity as stability).
